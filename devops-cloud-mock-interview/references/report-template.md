# End-of-Session Report Template

Fill this in with the real content from the session — don't leave placeholder-sounding text.
Use the candidate's chosen topic(s), difficulty, and company tier to frame the whole report, not
just the study plan section.

```markdown
## Mock Interview Report — [Topic(s)] · [Difficulty] · [Company tier]

### Overall
[2-3 sentences: overall readiness signal for this difficulty/tier combo. Be direct — "ready for
intermediate product-company interviews" or "not yet ready for Advanced/MANG-tier, here's why"
is more useful than a hedge.]

### Question-by-question

**Q1 — [topic]: [one-line restatement of the scenario]**
- Score: X/5
- What was strong: [specific]
- What was missing: [specific, and whether it's a knowledge gap or a reasoning/habit gap]
- Ideal answer would have covered: [bullet points]

[repeat per question]

### Topic-wise breakdown
| Topic | Signal |
|---|---|
| [topic] | Strong / Solid / Needs work — [one-line why] |

### Patterns across your answers
[Only include this if a real pattern showed up across 2+ answers — e.g. consistently skips
mentioning rollback/blast-radius, or consistently gives the right answer but under-explains
the "why." Don't force a pattern if the answers don't show one.]

### Study plan
Prioritized, most important first. Tie each item to the specific gap it addresses and to the
company tier the candidate is targeting.

1. [Gap] → [concrete action: specific concept to study, thing to practice, or type of question
   to drill — not just "study Kubernetes more"]
2. ...
```
